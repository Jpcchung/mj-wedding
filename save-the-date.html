Start planning your trip

Getting there

Fly into Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in Denpasar, Bali's only international airport. Most guests will connect through hubs like Singapore, Taipei, Seoul, Doha, or Sydney.

When to arrive

We suggest arriving by July 13 to settle in and joining us through July 16 or beyond. July is dry season in Bali: sunny, low humidity for the tropics, and around 27°C / 81°F. It's also peak season, so book flights early for the best fares.

Passports and visas

Your passport must be valid at least 6 months beyond your arrival date. Most nationalities (including US, Canada, UK, EU, and Australia) can get a visa on arrival or e-visa (e-VOA), valid 30 days. Check current requirements closer to travel.
