Getting There

Travel

Everything you need to reach us in Bali

✈

Flights

Everyone flies into Ngurah Rai International (DPS) in Denpasar; AYANA is about 12 km away, a 20 to 30 minute drive.

From Toronto (YYZ) there are no nonstops, so plan on one stop through a major hub. The smoothest single-connection routes are Cathay Pacific via Hong Kong, EVA Air via Taipei, Korean Air via Seoul, Japan Airlines or ANA via Tokyo, Qatar Airways via Doha, Emirates via Dubai, and Singapore Airlines via Singapore.

Total travel time runs roughly 23 to 30 hours depending on the layover. Bali is 11 to 12 hours ahead of Toronto, so build in a little time to adjust. July is peak season, so book early for the best fares.

🗄

Passport & visa

Passport valid 6+ months with blank pages. Most nationalities, including Canadians, qualify for visa on arrival or e-VOA (about IDR 500,000, 30 days). Apply online in advance at evisa.imigrasi.go.id to skip the queue.

Bali Tourist Levy: Every international tourist entering Bali pays a one-time IDR 150,000 tourism levy (roughly CAD $12 to $14, depending on exchange rates). This is separate from the visa fee.

⛅

Weather in July

Dry season and the loveliest time of year: sunny, around 27°C / 81°F, cooler evenings, little rain, with a touch of tropical humidity.

Questions

Everything you may be wondering

What are the travel requirements?

Passport: Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your arrival date, with a couple of blank pages.

Visa: Most nationalities, including Canadians, can get a visa on arrival or e-VOA for about IDR 500,000 (roughly CAD $45), valid 30 days. Apply online in advance at evisa.imigrasi.go.id to skip the queue.

Bali Tourist Levy: Every international visitor pays a one-time IDR 150,000 tourism levy (roughly CAD $12 to $14), separate from the visa fee. Pay online at lovebali.baliprov.go.id before you arrive.

Always double-check your country's current requirements before flying.

How do I get from the airport to the resort?

We recommend downloading Grab before your trip. It's Bali's equivalent of Uber and one of the easiest ways to get around the island.

AYANA is approximately 30 minutes by car from Bali's Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), depending on traffic. Grab has an official pickup area at the International Arrivals terminal, and you can book a car directly through the app once you arrive.

Expect to pay approximately IDR 150,000 to 300,000 ($13 to $25 CAD), depending on traffic, demand, and vehicle type. AYANA also offers private airport transfers if you prefer to have a driver waiting for you.

Will the wedding be indoors or outdoors?

Both our ceremony and reception will be outdoors, so dress for warm Bali weather and get ready to celebrate under the sun and stars.
