Getting There

Travel

Everything you need to reach us in Bali

✈

Flights

Fly into Ngurah Rai International (DPS), Denpasar. AYANA is 12 km away, a 20 to 30 minute drive. Popular connections through Singapore, Doha, Dubai, Seoul, Hong Kong and Sydney. July is peak season, so book early.

☼

When to arrive

We suggest arriving by Tuesday, July 13 and departing July 17 or later. Better yet, turn it into the holiday you deserve.

🗄

Passport & visa

Passport valid 6+ months with blank pages. Most nationalities qualify for visa on arrival or e-VOA (about IDR 500,000, 30 days). Apply online in advance to skip the queue.

¤

Money

Currency is Indonesian Rupiah. Cards work at resorts and restaurants; carry small cash for markets, drivers and temples. USD 1 is roughly IDR 16,000.

⛅

Weather in July

Dry season and the loveliest time of year: sunny, around 27°C / 81°F, cooler evenings, little rain, with a touch of tropical humidity.

📱

Time & connectivity

Bali runs on WITA (UTC+8). Grab an eSIM before you fly (Airalo, Holafly) or a local SIM at the airport. WiFi is excellent at every recommended hotel.

Good To Know

Questions

Everything you may be wondering

Do I need a visa?

Most nationalities can get a visa on arrival or e-VOA for about IDR 500,000 (roughly USD 30), valid 30 days. Apply online in advance to save time, and check your country's requirements before flying.

Can I bring children?

[Choose: "We adore your little ones, and this is an adults-only celebration" or "Children are warmly welcome, and AYANA has a wonderful kids club."]

Can I bring a plus one?

Invitations will be addressed with exact names. If yours says "and guest," absolutely.

Will transportation be provided?

Yes, a shuttle will run between recommended hotels and the venue on the wedding day. Grab and Gojek work beautifully otherwise.

What is the weather like?

July is Bali's dry season: sunny, around 27°C / 81°F, with very little rain. It's the finest month of the year to visit.

Are gifts expected?

Your presence in Bali is the greatest gift. If you'd still like to give something, see the Registry below.

Is Bali safe?

Yes, Bali is one of Asia's most visited destinations and very welcoming. Use normal travel sense and stick to registered taxis or Grab.

Can I drink the tap water?

No, drink bottled or filtered water only, which hotels provide free. Ice at hotels and restaurants is fine.

Do I need travel insurance?

We strongly recommend it, covering medical care and trip delays. Many policies exclude scooter riding, another reason to skip the scooter.

Are dietary needs accommodated?

Yes, the RSVP form asks about this, and AYANA's kitchens handle vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and halal beautifully.

What are the power outlets?

Type C and F (European two-pin), 230V. Bring a universal adapter.
